What to do first (any provider)

  1. Check for an outage in your provider's app.
  2. Check that every cable is tight and undamaged, and the power cord is in a working outlet.
  3. Make sure the equipment is not covered or in a hot, closed space.
  4. Unplug it for 30 seconds and plug it back in. Wait up to 15 minutes.
  5. If it stays red, contact your provider and tell them the exact light pattern.

Frequently asked questions

Is a red light on my router always bad?

A red light usually means a problem, but some are brief during startup. If it stays red 10 to 15 minutes after a restart, treat it as a problem.

Can I fix a red router light myself?

Often. Check cables, power and airflow and restart the equipment first. If it stays red, your provider needs to test the line or replace the unit.

Why is my router red but I still have internet?

Some red lights point to a secondary issue, like phone registration or overheating. Check your provider's light table.
