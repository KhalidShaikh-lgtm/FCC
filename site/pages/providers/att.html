AT&T: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

AT&T is a fiber, DSL and fixed wireless provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where AT&T reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, AT&T reports service in about 22,086 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (74%), across 49 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 21,862 ZIP codes with fixed wireless, 6,918 ZIP codes with DSL, 5,008 ZIP codes with fiber. The fastest speed reported anywhere is 5 Gbps download, but your address may get a lower tier or none at all.

Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with AT&T. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
