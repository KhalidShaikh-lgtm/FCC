Optimum: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Optimum is a cable and fiber provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Optimum reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Optimum reports service in about 1,634 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (6%), across 21 states. Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with Optimum. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
