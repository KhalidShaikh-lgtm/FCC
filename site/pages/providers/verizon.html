Verizon: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Verizon is a Fios fiber and 5G Home provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Verizon reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Verizon reports service in about 22,688 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (76%), across 49 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 22,057 ZIP codes with fixed wireless, 2,449 ZIP codes with fiber. The fastest speed reported anywhere is 2.3 Gbps download, but your address may get a lower tier or none at all.

Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with Verizon. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
