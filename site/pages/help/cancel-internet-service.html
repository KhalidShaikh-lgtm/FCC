How to Cancel Internet Service

Canceling is easier when you check the contract first, return equipment properly and keep proof that you canceled.

Cancel in order

  1. Check for a contract. Look for an early termination fee or promotional credits you could lose.
  2. Line up the new service first. If you are switching, get the new provider installed before you cancel the old one, so you are never without internet.
  3. Ask about retention offers. Providers often offer lower prices to keep customers. If you only want a cheaper price, see how to lower your internet bill.
  4. Cancel using the provider's official method and ask for a confirmation number and the cancellation date in writing.
  5. Return the equipment. Ask how and where, and keep the receipt or tracking number. Unreturned equipment is a common cause of extra charges.
  6. Check the final bill. Look for prorated charges and equipment fees, and dispute anything wrong.
  7. Stop automatic payments after the final bill is settled, and keep your records.

If you are moving, see moving your internet.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Will I be charged an early termination fee?

It depends on your contract. Many plans have no contract, but some promotions do.

What happens if I do not return the equipment?

Providers may charge an equipment fee. Return it and keep proof.

How far ahead should I cancel?

Cancel after the new service is working, and ask for the exact end date to avoid paying for extra days.

Can I cancel online?

It depends on the provider. Use its official channels and keep a confirmation.
