CenturyLink C4000 Modem: Lights, Reset and Fixes

The C4000 has one light ring on the front, and DSL models add a DSL light on the back. The ring works the same on every C4000 model (DSL C4000BG, BZ, LG, LZ and fiber C4000XG).

CenturyLinkModel guideChecked November 22, 2026

The C4000 has one light ring on the front, and DSL models add a DSL light on the back. The ring works the same on every C4000 model (DSL C4000BG, BZ, LG, LZ and fiber C4000XG).

What the lights mean

LightWhat it meansWhat to do
Blinking or solid blueBooting up and syncing with the network.
WAIT		Wait up to 5 minutes; if it keeps blinking, check the DSL cord at the modem and wall jack.
Solid greenOnline and connected.
OK		Nothing to do.
Fast-flickering greenSending or receiving data.
OK		Normal.
Blinking greenWPS is searching for a device to connect.
WAIT		Select your network on the device.
Blinking redWPS connection failed.
WAIT		Wait 2 minutes, check you chose the right network name (on the modem sticker), and retry.

ACTION		Reboot the modem first, then call or chat with CenturyLink.
Blinking amber-green or amber-whiteFirmware update in progress.
WAIT		Do not unplug it.

ACTION		Call or chat with CenturyLink.
OffNot connected to the network.
ACTION		Check power and the line, then reboot.

Rear DSL light

LightWhat it meansWhat to do
DSL light solid greenConnected with an active line (DSL models only).
OK		Nothing to do.
DSL light solid redConnection error.
ACTION		Call or chat with CenturyLink.
DSL light offNothing connected to the DSL port.
ACTION		Plug the DSL cord into the modem and wall jack.

Fix it step by step

  1. Check the front ring color against the table. Solid green means online.
  2. Solid red means a network failure. Reboot the modem first; CenturyLink calls rebooting the first step for a red ring.
  3. On DSL models, check the rear DSL light: red is a connection error, off means nothing is plugged into the DSL port.
  4. If the ring keeps blinking blue for more than 5 minutes, check that the green DSL cord is firmly in the modem and the wall jack; if it repeats, service may not be active on that jack, so try another jack.
  5. After a failed WPS pairing, wait 2 minutes for the ring to return to solid green, confirm the network name on the modem sticker, and retry.
  6. For severe errors, CenturyLink says a reset and repeat setup may be needed, ideally with a support agent.
Before any factory resetIt erases your Wi-Fi name, password and custom settings. Note them first, and reset only when the provider guide says to.
