Why did my Frontier bill go up?

What changed on Frontier bills, how to find the line that rose, and what to do about it.

Bill increaseSources listedChecked November 27, 2026

KSBy Khalid Shaikh, Head of Business Development· Checked November 27, 2026· LinkedIn

Short answer: Frontier bills usually go up when a promotional rate ends and the plan moves to the everyday rate, reported at $10 to $50 a month, or when a price guarantee ends because you changed plans. We found no Frontier price increase announced after Verizon completed its purchase on January 20, 2026; Frontier says plans stay the same for now.

How to find out why your Frontier bill went up

  1. Sign in to your Frontier account and compare the last two bills line by line: plan price, promotion or discount lines, equipment, fees and taxes.
  2. Find the date your promotion or price guarantee ends. If you changed plans after signing up, ask whether the guarantee still applies.
  3. Check that Autopay and paperless billing are still on, and that any Verizon mobile discount is still applied.

What to say when you call Frontier

Frontier's contact page directs customers to chat and lists 800-917-7489 for paying by phone and 800-921-8101 for sales. We did not find a Frontier-published billing or retention number, so use chat or the sign-in on frontier.com. Have your last two bills open. You are not asking for a favor; you are asking what the company will do to keep a customer who is paying more than a new customer would.

  1. Say: "My bill went from $___ to $___. Can you show me which lines changed?"
  2. Ask when your promotion or price guarantee ends and what the price is afterward.
  3. Ask: "What do new customers pay at my address for the same speed, and is a multi-year price guarantee available to me?" Ask what would end the guarantee, such as changing plans.
  4. Ask whether Autopay, paperless billing and any Verizon mobile discount are applied.
  5. If the price is still too high, say you are comparing other providers at your address and ask whether there is an offer for customers who are thinking of leaving.
  6. Get the new price, how long it lasts and what is excluded before you accept, and note the agent's name and the time.

Results vary and there is no guarantee of a discount.

You may not need to switch. If the new price is acceptable, or the call gets your bill back close to the old amount, staying put is a fine result. If you would rather stay with Frontier

Switching is usually where the bigger savings are, but if you want to stay, these steps can trim the bill:

  1. Before upgrading or downgrading, ask whether the change ends your price guarantee.
  2. If you also have Verizon mobile, ask about the Frontier and Verizon mobile discount.
  3. On DSL? Ask whether Frontier Fiber now reaches your address; fiber often costs about the same for far more speed.
  4. When you cancel, return all equipment to avoid the restocking and non-return charges.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my Frontier bill go up?

The most common reason is a promotion ending, so the plan moves to the everyday rate. Other causes are a price guarantee ending after a plan change, a lost Autopay or mobile discount, or an equipment or payment fee.

Did Frontier raise prices after Verizon bought it?

We found no announced Frontier residential price increase after the January 20, 2026 closing. Frontier says plans stay the same for now and that customers get advance notice of changes.

Can I lower my Frontier bill?

Ask what a new customer pays at your address for the same speed, ask whether a price guarantee is available, and check your Autopay and mobile discounts. If Frontier will not move, compare other providers at your ZIP code.
