Lost or Stolen SIM: What to Do Right Away

If your SIM or phone is lost or stolen, act quickly. Someone with your SIM can receive your calls and text messages, including verification codes.

Do these in order

  1. Call your carrier from another phone. Ask them to suspend or block the line and the SIM. You can also do this in your carrier's website or app from another device. Our provider support page lists official numbers.
  2. Use a find-my-phone tool. If the phone is lost, try your phone's find, lock and erase options before you give up on it.
  3. Request a replacement SIM or eSIM. Ask your carrier for a new SIM or an eSIM transfer, and confirm your number is moved to it.
  4. Add a port-out or account PIN. Ask your carrier to add a PIN to your account so no one can move your number to another carrier or SIM.
  5. Secure important accounts. Change passwords for email, banking and social accounts, and move two-factor codes to an authenticator app instead of text messages.
  6. Watch your bank and credit accounts. If your phone is stolen, tell your bank and watch for unfamiliar activity.
  7. Report a theft. If it was stolen, you can file a police report, which some carriers or insurers may ask for.
  8. Ask about insurance or a replacement phone if you have device protection.

Keep your account number and the last bill handy when you call, and ask the carrier to note on your account that the line was lost or stolen.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone use my phone number if they have my SIM?

They may be able to receive calls and texts, including verification codes, until the line is suspended. Suspend it quickly.

Will I keep my phone number with a new SIM?

Yes. Your carrier can move your number to a new SIM or eSIM after verifying your identity.

Is a SIM replacement free?

It varies by carrier and situation. Ask when you contact them.

What is a port-out PIN?

It is a security code on your mobile account that prevents your number from being transferred without it.
