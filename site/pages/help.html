Internet, TV and Mobile Help Guides

Plain-language fixes for the most common internet, TV and mobile problems.

  • Internet Not Working: What to Do - Step-by-step fixes when your home internet is down: check outages, restart your modem and router, test cables, and know when to call your provider.
  • Slow Internet: Causes and Fixes - Why your internet is slow and how to fix it: test your speed, move closer to the router, cut device load, upgrade old equipment, or change plans.
  • Wi-Fi Keeps Disconnecting - Fix a Wi-Fi connection that keeps dropping: interference, router placement, channel settings, outdated firmware and when to use a mesh system.
  • TV Not Working: No Signal or Black Screen - Fix a TV with no signal or a frozen cable or streaming box: check the input, restart the box, test HDMI and cables, and confirm your account is active.
  • TV Channels Missing: ESPN, Showtime, Starz and More - Why TV channels are missing or locked, and how premium channels like Showtime and Starz differ from ESPN: packages, add-ons, signal refresh and streaming options.
  • Lost or Stolen SIM: What to Do Right Away - Steps if your SIM card or phone is lost or stolen: suspend the line, protect your accounts, get a replacement SIM or eSIM, and set a port-out PIN.
  • How to Set Up a Modem and Router - How to install and set up your own modem and router: connect cables, activate the modem, change the Wi-Fi name and password and place the router for the best signal.
  • Self-Install vs Professional Install - Compare self-install and professional installation for home internet: cost, time, when each makes sense and a checklist for installation day.
  • How to Cancel Internet Service - Steps to cancel home internet or TV: check your contract, return equipment, get a confirmation and avoid extra charges on your final bill.
  • Moving Your Internet Service - Moving soon? How to transfer or start home internet at a new address: check availability by ZIP, book installation early and avoid a gap in service.
