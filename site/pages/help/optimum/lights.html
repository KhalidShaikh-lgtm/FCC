Optimum Router Lights: What Each Light Means

Red light? Blinking? Here is what each Optimum status light means and exactly what to do about it, from Optimum's own support documents.

OptimumOfficial sourcesChecked November 22, 2026

Use this guide to read the status lights on your Optimum modem and router. Find the light you see in the first column. Colors are shown as dots (blinking dots flash). Exact lights differ by model, so check the label on your equipment too.

Optimum modem, router, Altice One and extender lights

LightWhat it meansWhat to do
Modem: four solid lightsThe modem is online.
OK		Plug the router in after the modem shows four solid lights.
Arris TM802/TM822: four green lights and a flashing amber fifthModem is ready during setup.
WAIT		This can take a few minutes.
Arris TM1602: three solid blue lights and a flashing green WAN port lightModem is ready during setup.
WAIT		Continue setup.
Smart Router: two front lights flash green togetherThe router has finished updating and is ready.
OK		Nothing to do.
Altice One standby light: whiteThe box is on and ready.
OK		Nothing to do.
Altice One standby light: redThe box is off.
ACTION		Press the power button on the box.
Extender 6/6e Wi-Fi light: solid whiteGood signal.
OK		Nothing to do.
Extender 6/6e: solid red or flashing whiteNot enough signal.
ACTION		Move the extender closer to the gateway.
Extender 6/6e: flashing redThe extender needs to be reconnected.
ACTION		Re-pair it to the gateway.
Quick ruleSolid and steady is usually good. Blinking at startup is normal. A red or off light that lasts more than 10 to 15 minutes after a restart usually means a cable, power, signal or equipment problem.

Frequently asked questions

What does a red light on my Optimum equipment mean?

It usually means a problem with the connection or hardware. See the table above for your exact pattern, then restart the equipment. If it stays red, contact Optimum.

What does a blinking light mean on Optimum?

Blinking is normal for a few minutes at startup or while updating. If it keeps blinking for 10 to 15 minutes after a restart, check cables and contact Optimum.

How do I restart my Optimum Optimum modem and router?

Unplug the power cord, wait 30 seconds and plug it back in. See our Optimum troubleshooting guide for model-specific reset steps.
