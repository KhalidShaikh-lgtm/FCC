Rocket Money bill negotiation alternatives

What changed on November 2, 2026, what the other services charge, and the two options that cost nothing.

ComparisonFees checkedSources listedChecked November 26, 2026

KSBy Khalid Shaikh, Head of Business Development· Checked November 26, 2026· LinkedIn

Short answer: Since November 2, 2026, Rocket Money only negotiates bills for Premium+ members at $15 a month plus tax, with no added success fee. The main alternatives are Billshark (40% of savings), BillCutterz (50%), Experian BillFixer (included in a paid membership), calling your provider yourself, or switching providers, which carries no fee on your savings.

What changed at Rocket Money

Rocket Money's help center says that as of November 2, 2026, bill negotiation is available only to members on Premium+, a fixed $15 a month plus tax. In return there is no added success fee on negotiations you submit as a Premium+ member. You can have one negotiation in progress at a time.

Before the change, Rocket Money charged a success fee on each lowered bill. BestMoney reports the old fee as 35% to 60% of the savings. Rocket Money says negotiations submitted before November 2 finish under the old terms.

So the question is now simple: is one lowered bill worth $15 a month to you, for as long as you keep the membership?

Which one fits your situation

  • You have several bills to negotiate this year. A flat membership (Rocket Money Premium+ or Experian) can cost less than paying 40% to 50% on each bill.
  • You have one bill and do not want a subscription. A pay-on-success service such as Billshark or BillCutterz charges only if it lowers the bill, but it keeps a large share of the saving.
  • Your promotional price just ended. Call your provider and ask for the retention team. Our scripts for Xfinity, Spectrum, AT&T and Cox cost nothing to use.
  • Another provider serves your address. New-customer offers are often lower than a long-time customer's price, so switching can save more than a negotiated discount, and nobody takes a cut. See what to do when your promo ends.

What to check before you hand over a bill

Complaints filed with the Better Business Bureau about negotiation services tend to repeat the same points: fees that customers did not expect, savings figures they dispute, and negotiations that complainants say they did not authorize. Both Rocket Money and Billshark hold an A+ BBB rating, and both answer complaints, but the themes are worth knowing.

  • Ask how the saving is calculated and over how many months.
  • Ask whether the service may change your plan, speed or contract length.
  • Check whether the service will renegotiate automatically later.
  • Check your next two bills to confirm the new price is really there.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rocket Money bill negotiation still free to try?

No. Since November 2, 2026, Rocket Money's help center says bill negotiation is available only with a Premium+ membership, which costs $15 a month plus tax.

Does Rocket Money still take a percentage of my savings?

Not on new negotiations. Rocket Money says Premium+ members pay no added success fee. Negotiations submitted before November 2, 2026 finish under the old success-fee terms.

What is the cheapest alternative to Rocket Money bill negotiation?

Calling your provider yourself or switching to another provider at your address. Neither takes a fee from your savings, though a new provider may charge for installation. Paid services charge a membership or 40% to 50% of the saving.

Does YourBillCheck negotiate my bill?

No. YourBillCheck compares your bill with offers from other providers at your address and can help you switch by phone. It is free and takes no share of your savings.
