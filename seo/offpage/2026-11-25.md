## Next link-worthy asset: "Internet Bill Fee Index 2026"
A data study built from the FCC broadband labels that the big providers must publish. For the 10-15 largest ISPs, list the advertised price against the full monthly cost with every required fee, equipment charge and post-promo price. Rank them by "promo-to-real" gap, and update it every quarter. Why it earns links: journalists covering price increases (#3, #6, #10 above) need one citable number per provider, and consumer offices (#5) can link it as a neutral reference.
