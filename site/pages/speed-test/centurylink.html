Frequently asked questions

How do I test my CenturyLink internet speed?

Press GO on this page with a device connected to your CenturyLink network, ideally by Ethernet, and compare the result with your plan.

Why is my CenturyLink speed slower than advertised?

Wi-Fi, distance from the router, busy devices and peak hours all reduce speed. Test by cable to see the line speed.

Is this the official CenturyLink speed test?

No. It is an independent test and is not affiliated with CenturyLink. It uses Cloudflare's open speed test engine.
