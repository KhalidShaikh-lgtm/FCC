How to Move Xfinity Service

Step-by-step: transfer Xfinity service to your new address, who to call and what to ask.

XfinityMovingChecked Nov 2026Checked November 22, 2026

You can usually move Xfinity service if your new address is inside Xfinity's service area. If it isn't, you will need a different provider at the new home. Either way, start 2 to 4 weeks before you move.

How to move or transfer Xfinity service

  1. Check the new address. Enter the new ZIP code on our site to see if Xfinity is listed, then confirm the exact address with Xfinity. Coverage can change from street to street.
  2. Contact Xfinity. Use the official numbers below. Say that you are moving, give the new address and your move date.
  3. Ask what applies to you. Ask about any transfer, installation or activation fee, whether you can self-install, and whether your current plan and price carry over.
  4. Ask about equipment. Ask whether to bring your modem or gateway, return it, or use new equipment at the new address.
  5. Book the date. Pick an activation or install date close to move-in day and write down the confirmation number.
  6. Set up and test. Plug in at the new home, wait for the lights to settle, then run a speed test. If a light looks wrong, see Xfinity troubleshooting.

If Xfinity does not serve your new address

Compare the providers that do. See provider comparisons, then check the new ZIP code below. Keep your old service until the new one is working, so you are never offline on moving day.

Moving checklist

  1. 4 to 6 weeks before
    Find out who serves the new address. Enter the new ZIP code on our site, then confirm the exact address on the provider's own site or by phone. Availability can differ from house to house.
  2. 2 to 4 weeks before
    Decide: transfer your current service if your provider serves the new address, or sign up with a different provider. Book the install or activation date, ideally on or just before move-in day.
  3. 1 to 2 weeks before
    Confirm the date and any equipment instructions: whether to bring your modem or gateway, return it, or use new gear. Ask about transfer, install or activation fees, and keep a note of the confirmation number.
  4. Moving day
    Keep your old service on until the new connection works. Pack the modem, router and power cords in a clearly labelled box so they are the first thing you set up.
  5. First day in the new home
    Plug in, wait for the lights to settle, and run a speed test. If something is wrong, use our troubleshooter to match your lights to what they mean.
  6. After the move
    Cancel or finish the old service, return any equipment the provider asks for, and check that your final bill is prorated the way you were told.

Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer my Xfinity service to a new address?

Usually yes, if the new address is within Xfinity's service area. If it is not, you will need a different provider. Contact Xfinity with your new address and move date to confirm.

How far ahead should I contact Xfinity about moving?

Two to four weeks before your move gives enough time to check availability and book an install or activation date.

Should I take my Xfinity equipment with me?

It depends on the provider and your plan. Ask Xfinity whether to bring, return or replace your modem or gateway when you call.
