Moving Your Internet Service

Providers are not the same at every address, so check what is available at your new home before you move.

Plan your move

  1. Check availability at the new address. Enter the new ZIP on our home page to see which providers report service, then confirm the exact address with the provider.
  2. Start 2 to 3 weeks early. Installation appointments can book up, especially at the end of the month.
  3. Ask about transferring service if your current provider serves the new address. You may keep your plan or get a new offer.
  4. Compare new customer offers. A different provider may offer a lower price or faster speeds at the new address.
  5. Schedule the cancellation at the old address to match your move date.
  6. Return old equipment and keep the receipt.
  7. On move-in day, test the connection with our speed test and keep your order number handy.

See how to cancel and self-install vs professional install.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take my internet provider with me when I move?

Only if the provider serves your new address. Check by ZIP and confirm with the provider.

When should I order internet for a new home?

Two to three weeks before you move is a good rule.

Is it better to switch providers when I move?

It can be, since new customers often get lower promotional prices. Compare the options at the new address.

What if my new address has only one provider?

You may have fewer choices, but you can still compare plans, speeds and price after the promotion.
