## Facts every pitch may use (all from the page)
- 34.1% of 29,663 ZIPs (10,113) have exactly one wired provider with meaningful reach (50% threshold). 27.0% at a 25% threshold, 54.9% at 80%.
- 5.1% (1,504) have none. 37.6% (11,146) have no meaningful fiber.
- Where there is one wired provider: fiber 47.2%, cable 35.7%, DSL 17.1%. 13.8% top out under 100 Mbps.
- Highest share: North Dakota 78.3% (296 of 378), South Dakota 71.8% (250 of 348), Montana 65.2% (195 of 299), Kansas 51.7% (351 of 679).
- Highest count: New York 678 (40.7%), Pennsylvania 578 (39.5%), Texas 496 (28.5%).
- Lone provider by ZIP count: Spectrum 1,594, Xfinity 1,178, Frontier 380.
- Limits (state them in every pitch): provider-reported availability, area around ZIP center, wired only, not household-weighted.
