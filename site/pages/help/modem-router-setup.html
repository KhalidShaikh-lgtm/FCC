How to Set Up a Modem and Router

Setting up your internet takes about 20 minutes with the right steps. Use the equipment from your provider or your own compatible modem and router.

Set it up in order

  1. Choose a spot. Put the modem where the cable or fiber line comes in, and place the router in a central, open area.
  2. Connect the line. Attach the coax or phone line to the modem, or connect the fiber terminal as your provider instructs.
  3. Connect the modem to the router with an Ethernet cable, then plug in power to the modem first and then the router.
  4. Wait for the lights. The modem can take several minutes to connect. Wait until the online light is steady.
  5. Activate service. Follow your provider's activation steps in its app, website or by phone, if the modem needs to be activated.
  6. Name your Wi-Fi and set a strong password. Use a long password that you do not use anywhere else.
  7. Update the firmware in the router app, and turn on automatic updates.
  8. Test your speed with our speed test and compare it with your plan.

Not sure whether to set it up yourself? See self-install vs professional install.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy my own modem?

Not always. Many providers rent equipment for a monthly fee, but you can often use your own compatible equipment. Check your provider's approved list.

Why is my new modem not connecting?

It may need to be activated, or the cable may be loose. Wait several minutes and check your provider's activation steps.

Where should I put the router?

In a central, open spot, away from walls, metal and microwaves.

Should I change the default Wi-Fi password?

Yes. A strong, unique password keeps others off your network.
