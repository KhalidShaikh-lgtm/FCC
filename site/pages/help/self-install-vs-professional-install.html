Self-Install vs Professional Install

Many providers let you choose between a self-install kit and a technician. Here is how to decide.

How to choose

  1. Self-install fits when the line is already active at your address, such as cable or fiber that was used before. It is usually quicker and often free or low cost.
  2. Professional install fits when the address is new to the provider, wiring is damaged or missing, or you want help with fiber hookups and Wi-Fi coverage.
  3. Ask about the cost. Fees vary by provider and offer, and some waive installation with a promotion. Ask whether a self-install kit or a technician visit has a fee.
  4. Ask for the appointment window and whether someone over 18 must be home.
  5. Before the visit, clear a path to where the equipment will go, know where you want the router and ask where the technician will drill, if at all.
  6. After the visit, run a speed test, check Wi-Fi in each room and keep the work order number.

If you rent a home, check with your landlord before drilling or running new lines.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Is self-install cheaper?

Often yes, but it depends on the provider and offer.

How long does a technician install take?

It commonly takes a few hours, depending on the address and the type of service.

Can I do a fiber install myself?

Some providers allow it if the fiber line is already at your home. Others require a technician. Ask your provider.

What should I do if the speed is lower after the install?

Test with Ethernet, restart the equipment, and ask the provider to recheck if the wired speed is far below your plan.
