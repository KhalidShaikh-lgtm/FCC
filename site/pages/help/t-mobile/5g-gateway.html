T-Mobile 5G Gateway Gen5: Lights, Reset and Fixes

The T-Mobile 5G Gateway Gen5 has an LCD screen instead of colored lights. You use the arrow buttons to move through screens that show signal, connected devices and messages.

T-MobileModel guideChecked November 22, 2026

The T-Mobile 5G Gateway Gen5 has an LCD screen instead of colored lights. You use the arrow buttons to move through screens that show signal, connected devices and messages.

What the screen shows

ScreenWhat it shows
ConnectionUp to five bars show cellular signal strength. An X means the gateway is not connected to the cellular network.
DevicesHow many wired and wireless devices are connected. Use the T-Life app to manage each device.
Internet Line NumberShows your internet number when a SIM card is installed.
MessagesLets you read messages from T-Mobile.
LanguageEnglish and Español.

Fix it step by step

  1. On the Connection screen, count the bars. An X means the gateway is not connected to the cellular network.
  2. Move the gateway to a spot with stronger signal (near a window, higher up, away from large metal objects). T-Mobile notes the gateway operates between 32°F and 104°F.
  3. Restart it: unplug the gateway for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait 2 minutes before testing.
  4. T-Mobile installs firmware updates automatically between 1 and 3 a.m. Pacific, so leave the gateway powered on then and do not restart it during an update.
  5. Use the Devices screen or the T-Life app to see how many devices are connected.
  6. Turn off any VPN or firewall and restart connected devices. If the X stays, contact T-Mobile Home Internet support.
Before any factory resetIt erases your Wi-Fi name, password and custom settings. Note them first, and reset only when the provider guide says to.
