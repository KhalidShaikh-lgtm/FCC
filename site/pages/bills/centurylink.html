Why is my CenturyLink bill so high?

What pushes a CenturyLink bill up, which charges you can remove, and what to say to get a lower rate.

Short answerCenturyLink internet is mostly priced month to month, but leased equipment and other charges are not price-protected and can rise. Its Quantum Fiber business was sold to AT&T in 2026.

What happens to your CenturyLink bill after the promotion

CenturyLink and Quantum Fiber have offered 'Price for Life' on the internet rate. CenturyLink says other rates, fees and charges on the account, including leased equipment, are not protected and may increase. Leased modem and Wi-Fi equipment has been billed at about $15 a month in Quantum Fiber offers.

For comparison, CenturyLink's typical new-customer starting price we track is from $50/mo (November 2026). If you pay a lot more for similar speed, you are probably on post-promotion pricing or paying add-ons.

How to lower your CenturyLink bill

  1. Check whether you are leasing a modem; your own compatible modem removes that line.
  2. On DSL? Ask whether fiber is now available at your address. It is often similar money for far more speed.
  3. Remove home phone features you no longer use.
  4. If you switch, return leased equipment within 30 days.

Before you call: 6-point bill check

  1. Find the date your promotion or price guarantee ends.
  2. Compare your plan price with the price new customers get in your ZIP code today.
  3. List every line on your bill: equipment rental, Wi-Fi, TV fees, add-ons, protection plans.
  4. Check whether you pay for speed you don't use. Run a speed test and look at how many people stream at once.
  5. Turn on Auto Pay and paperless billing if they lower your price.
  6. Call retention with a specific price in mind, or switch if another provider serves your address for less.

Asking CenturyLink for a better rate

What to say when you call"Hi, my bill has gone up to $[your total]. I can see new customers in my area are offered [speed] for $[price]. I'd like to stay, but I need a lower rate. What can you offer? If there's nothing today, please transfer me to the cancellations or retention team."

Ask for the retention or loyalty team. They usually have offers the first person you speak to does not. Be polite, be specific about the number you want, and write down the rep's name and the new price before you hang up.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my CenturyLink bill go up?

CenturyLink internet is mostly priced month to month, but leased equipment and other charges are not price-protected and can rise. Its Quantum Fiber business was sold to AT&T in 2026.

Can I get CenturyLink to lower my bill?

Often, yes. Call CenturyLink, ask for the retention or loyalty team, and quote the price new customers get in your area. If they will not match it, compare other providers at your address.

Does a CenturyLink price guarantee cover everything?

No. Price guarantees and price locks cover the base plan rate. Taxes, regulatory fees and equipment charges are usually excluded and can still rise.

Is it worth buying my own modem?

If your plan charges a monthly equipment rental, a compatible modem you own usually pays for itself within a year. Check your provider's approved device list first.
