TV Channels Missing: ESPN, Showtime, Starz and More

Channels can disappear because of your package, an add-on that is not active, a signal issue or a lineup change. Here is how to tell which one it is.

Find out why a channel is missing

  1. Check your package. Sign in to your account and see which package and add-ons you have. Many channels only come with higher tiers.
  2. Check premium add-ons. Channels like Showtime, Starz and HBO are usually premium add-ons that cost extra, even if your base package includes many others.
  3. Check sports packages. Where ESPN and other sports channels appear depends on your provider and tier, and regional sports networks may be in separate packages.
  4. Look for a lineup change. Providers and networks sometimes lose or add channels after a contract dispute, which affects everyone in that area.
  5. Refresh the box. Restart it, or ask your provider to send a refresh signal to re-authorize your channels.
  6. Check the channel number. Channel numbers differ by area and provider, so use the guide on the box or the provider's channel lineup page.
  7. Contact your provider. If you pay for a channel that is not working, ask them to re-authorize it, and check your next bill for credits.

Streaming apps and premium channels

Several premium channels also sell their own streaming apps, which can work with any internet connection. Compare the cost of the add-on on your TV package with the standalone app price before deciding. Prices and bundles change often, so check each service's current price.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Why are some channels blocked on my TV?

The channel may be in a package or add-on you do not have, or it may need to be re-authorized by your provider.

Is ESPN included in every TV package?

No. Which tiers include ESPN and other sports channels varies by provider, so check your package or the provider's channel lineup.

Are Showtime and Starz included?

Usually they are premium add-ons that cost extra, though some promotions include them for a period.

Can I watch premium channels without a cable package?

Many premium channels offer their own streaming apps. Check each service's current pricing.
