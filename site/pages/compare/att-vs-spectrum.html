AT&T vs Spectrum: Which Internet Provider Is Better?

Both AT&T and Spectrum sell home internet, but they are not always available at the same address. Here is how they compare on coverage, speed and price.

Availability: who can you actually get?

Of the 29,663 ZIP codes in our data, 7,620 have both AT&T and Spectrum reporting service, 14,466 have only AT&T, and 1,045 have only Spectrum. That means in many places you will only have one of the two, and the better choice is simply whichever one serves your address. Enter your ZIP on the home page to see which of them report service where you live.

Speed and connection type

AT&T is mostly fiber, DSL and fixed wireless; Spectrum is mostly cable and fiber. Fiber usually gives the most consistent speeds and upload performance, cable is widely available and fast on download, and DSL or fixed wireless can be slower and vary more by address. In the data, AT&T's top reported speed is 5 Gbps and Spectrum's is 1 Gbps, but that is the best tier in the best place, not what every address gets.

Price

AT&T: from $35/mo. $100 reward card with AT&T Fiber 1 Gig or faster. Spectrum: from $30/mo. 300 Mbps for $30/mo with Auto Pay, plus free Advanced WiFi and a free mobile line for 1 year. Watch for what happens when the promotional price ends, equipment fees, data caps and whether you need AutoPay or a bundle to get the low price.

AT&T: Promo price for new customers with AutoPay and paperless billing, for 12 months. The regular price is higher.

How to choose

  • If only one of them serves your address, that decides it. Check both pages: AT&T and Spectrum.
  • If both serve you, compare the total first-year cost including equipment and fees, not just the headline monthly price.
  • Pick the faster tier only if you need it. Most homes do well with 300 to 500 Mbps; see how much speed you need.
  • Ask for the price you will pay in month 13, and whether there is a contract or early termination fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is AT&T or Spectrum better?

It depends on which one serves your address and what you pay after the promotion. In our data both report service in 7,620 ZIP codes; elsewhere you may only have one.

Which is cheaper, AT&T or Spectrum?

AT&T lists from $35/mo and Spectrum lists from $30/mo for new customers. Compare the first-year total with equipment and fees.

Can I get AT&T and Spectrum at the same address?

Sometimes. 7,620 ZIP codes in our data have both reporting service, but availability is by address, so check each provider.

Which has faster internet, AT&T or Spectrum?

The top reported speeds are 5 Gbps for AT&T and 1 Gbps for Spectrum, but your address determines which speeds you can actually order.
