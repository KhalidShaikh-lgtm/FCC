Xfinity: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Xfinity is a cable and fiber provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Xfinity reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Xfinity reports service in about 7,343 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (25%), across 40 states.
