How to read your results

  • Download is how fast data reaches you: streaming, browsing, downloads.
  • Upload is how fast you send data: video calls, cloud backups, posting video.
  • Latency is the delay in milliseconds. Lower is better for gaming and calls.
  • Jitter is how much latency varies. High jitter makes calls and games choppy.

How to get an accurate test

  1. Use a computer connected by Ethernet if you can.
  2. Close streaming, downloads and cloud backups.
  3. Run it two or three times and at different times of day.
  4. Compare the result with the speed on your bill. Wi-Fi results are usually lower than wired.

This test runs in your browser using Cloudflare's open speed test and does not need an account. Results depend on your device, Wi-Fi and the time of day.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good internet speed?

For most homes, 100 to 500 Mbps download is comfortable. Basic use works with 25 to 100 Mbps. See our guide on how much speed you need.

Why is my speed test lower than my plan?

Wi-Fi, old equipment, other devices and time of day all lower results. Test with Ethernet to see your true connection speed.

Is this speed test free?

Yes, it is free and needs no account.

How often should I test my internet speed?

Test when you notice slowdowns, after changing equipment and before calling your provider.
