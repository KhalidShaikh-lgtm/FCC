# yourbillcheck.com roadmap: billing-first (set 2026-11-27)

**Mission.** A free watchdog for household bills. We show people why their bill went up, how much they are overpaying,
and how to get it back down: by calling their provider, by changing plan, or, only when it really saves money, by switching.
Revenue comes from the calls where switching or a better plan saves them money.

**Why.** Search Console Nov 21-26: billing pages are 3% of pages but 62% of clicks (CTR 0.83%, position 8.9) versus
city/ZIP pages (CTR 0.03%, position 37). The big comparison sites own "providers near me"; the bill-problem space is open.

## Goals (check every Monday in the weekly run)
| By | Goal |
|---|---|
| Dec 15, 2026 | Spectrum + Xfinity went-up pages in top 5; went-up pages for 12+ providers; alert signup live on all of them |
| Jan 31, 2027 | 300+ weekly clicks from billing pages; price tracker updated monthly; loyalty-penalty study published and pitched |
| Feb 28, 2027 | Calls + leads every week from billing pages; 100+ alert subscribers; at least one press mention or link |

## Workstreams
1. **Rank what already ranks** (now): title/meta rewrites and fuller answers on pages at positions 5-15
   (Spectrum went-up, price-increases guide, why-bill-went-up, Xfinity went-up dollar amounts).
2. **Cover every provider**: went-up, lower-your-bill and read-your-bill pages for the top 15 providers (internet, TV, wireless).
3. **Retention scripts**: what to say to the retention department, per provider. The honest advice that builds trust.
4. **Price Hike Tracker + alerts**: monthly dated tracker (confirmed vs reported) and email alerts by provider.
5. **Data for press**: loyalty-penalty study, monthly price index. Pitches drafted only; Khalid approves every send.
6. **Funnel**: "Did your bill go up?" box on city/ZIP pages, bill-reader and promo-reminder tools linked from every billing page.
