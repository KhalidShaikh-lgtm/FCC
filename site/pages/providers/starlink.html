Starlink: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Starlink is a satellite provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Starlink reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Starlink reports service in about 29,648 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (100%), across 51 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 29,648 ZIP codes with satellite. The fastest speed reported anywhere is 280 Mbps download, but your address may get a lower tier or none at all.

Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with Starlink. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
