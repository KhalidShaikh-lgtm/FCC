TV Not Working: No Signal or Black Screen

A TV that shows no signal or a black screen usually points to the wrong input, a cable problem or a box that needs a restart.

Checked November 22, 2026

Fix it step by step

  1. Check the input. Make sure the TV is set to the HDMI input your box is connected to.
  2. Check power. Confirm both the TV and the cable or streaming box are on and the lights are normal.
  3. Test the HDMI cable. Reseat it at both ends, or try another cable or another HDMI port.
  4. Restart the box. Unplug it for 30 seconds, plug it back in and wait a few minutes for it to start.
  5. Check the remote. Replace the batteries and make sure the remote is paired with the right device.
  6. Check the coax cable. Make sure the cable from the wall is tight at both ends. A loose connector can cause pixelation or lost channels.
  7. Check for an outage or account issue in your provider's app or website.
  8. Try a different TV or port to see whether the TV or the box is at fault.
  9. Contact your provider to ask them to refresh the box signal, replace the box or schedule a technician.

For missing channels on a working picture, see channels missing. If your internet is also down, see internet not working.

Provider support numbers

If these steps do not fix it, call your provider using the number on your bill or on its own site. We list official numbers for the largest providers on our provider support page.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my TV say no signal?

The TV may be on the wrong input, the cable may be loose, or the box may need a restart.

Why is my cable box frozen?

A restart often fixes it. Unplug it for 30 seconds and plug it back in.

Can a bad HDMI cable cause a black screen?

Yes. Try another cable or port.

Will my provider send a new box?

Often, if a restart does not fix it. Ask your provider about replacement options.
