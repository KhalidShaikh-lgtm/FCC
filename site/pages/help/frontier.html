Frontier Troubleshooting: Lights, Restart and Fixes

Frontier fiber ONT and router. Fix a red light, no internet or a dropped connection step by step, using Frontier's own support guidance.

Frontier: no internet or a red light

  1. Check for an outage in your area and restart your router first.
  2. Make sure the router is plugged directly into a wall outlet (not a power strip) and the power light is green. Check the router's Internet light: if it is off, red or amber, you have a connection problem.
  3. Find your ONT, the box where fiber enters your home, usually in a garage, basement or outside near the power box. Confirm its lights are on and it has power.
  4. If the house has power but the ONT does not, check whether it is on a GFCI outlet. Unplug the ONT, test the outlet with a lamp, and if it is dead press the outlet's reset button.
  5. Restart the ONT by unplugging it for at least 5 minutes (see below), then wait at least 5 minutes for service to return.
  6. If it still fails, contact Frontier through chat or its help center.

How to restart your Frontier equipment

  1. Router: press the power button or unplug the cord, wait 30 seconds, then turn it back on. The globe/internet light glowing means the reset has finished.
  2. Factory reset: press and hold the router's Reset button for 10 seconds with a pen or paper clip. This erases settings.
  3. ONT: unplug it, wait at least 5 minutes, plug it back in. If it has a battery backup, disconnect one battery lead first and reconnect it 30 seconds after plugging the ONT back in.
  4. Keep the router on at all times if you have Frontier Fiber TV; the TV needs it.
Before you factory resetA factory reset erases your Wi-Fi name, password and custom settings. Write them down first, and only reset when your provider or its guide tells you to.

Frontier TV remote and set-top box

Frontier says to keep your router plugged in and on at all times, even if you are not using the internet, because Fiber TV needs it. To fix a remote button that does not work, hold OK and Live TV (or OK and star) together until the red light blinks twice and stays on, then follow the on-screen steps. Check the ONT or splitter connections if you think a connection is loose, and make sure your TV is on the right input.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Frontier internet not working?

The most common causes are an outage, a loose cable, or equipment that needs a restart. Check the lights on your Frontier router and ONT, check for an outage in the Frontier app, and restart the equipment.

How do I reset my Frontier Frontier router and ONT?

Router: press the power button or unplug the cord, wait 30 seconds, then turn it back on. The globe/internet light glowing means the reset has finished.

How long should I wait after restarting Frontier equipment?

Allow a few minutes. Some equipment needs up to 15 minutes to reconnect and finish updates.
