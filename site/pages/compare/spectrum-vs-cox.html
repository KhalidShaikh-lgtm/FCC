Spectrum vs Cox: Which Internet Provider Is Better?

Both Spectrum and Cox sell home internet, but they are not always available at the same address. Here is how they compare on coverage, speed and price.

Availability: who can you actually get?

Of the 29,663 ZIP codes in our data, 70 have both Spectrum and Cox reporting service, 8,595 have only Spectrum, and 1,138 have only Cox. That means in many places you will only have one of the two, and the better choice is simply whichever one serves your address. Enter your ZIP on the home page to see which of them report service where you live.

Speed and connection type

Spectrum is mostly cable and fiber; Cox is mostly cable. Fiber usually gives the most consistent speeds and upload performance, cable is widely available and fast on download, and DSL or fixed wireless can be slower and vary more by address. In the data, Spectrum's top reported speed is 1 Gbps and Cox's is 2 Gbps, but that is the best tier in the best place, not what every address gets.

Price

Spectrum: from $30/mo. 300 Mbps for $30/mo with Auto Pay, plus free Advanced WiFi and a free mobile line for 1 year. Cox: from $55/mo. Watch for what happens when the promotional price ends, equipment fees, data caps and whether you need AutoPay or a bundle to get the low price.

Cox: Cox plans include a 1.25 TB monthly data cap.

How to choose

  • If only one of them serves your address, that decides it. Check both pages: Spectrum and Cox.
  • If both serve you, compare the total first-year cost including equipment and fees, not just the headline monthly price.
  • Pick the faster tier only if you need it. Most homes do well with 300 to 500 Mbps; see how much speed you need.
  • Ask for the price you will pay in month 13, and whether there is a contract or early termination fee.

Support numbers

Both providers list support numbers on their own sites; we collect them on each provider page: Spectrum support and Cox support.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spectrum or Cox better?

It depends on which one serves your address and what you pay after the promotion. In our data both report service in 70 ZIP codes; elsewhere you may only have one.

Which is cheaper, Spectrum or Cox?

Spectrum lists from $30/mo and Cox lists from $55/mo for new customers. Compare the first-year total with equipment and fees.

Can I get Spectrum and Cox at the same address?

Sometimes. 70 ZIP codes in our data have both reporting service, but availability is by address, so check each provider.

Which has faster internet, Spectrum or Cox?

The top reported speeds are 1 Gbps for Spectrum and 2 Gbps for Cox, but your address determines which speeds you can actually order.
