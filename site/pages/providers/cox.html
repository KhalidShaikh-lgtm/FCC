Cox: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Cox is a cable provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Cox reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Cox reports service in about 1,208 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (4%), across 17 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 1,140 ZIP codes with cable, 445 ZIP codes with fiber, 5 ZIP codes with fixed wireless. The fastest speed reported anywhere is 2 Gbps download, but your address may get a lower tier or none at all.

Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with Cox. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
