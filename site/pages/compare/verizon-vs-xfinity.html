Verizon vs Xfinity: Which Internet Provider Is Better?

Both Verizon and Xfinity sell home internet, but they are not always available at the same address. Here is how they compare on coverage, speed and price.

Availability: who can you actually get?

Of the 29,663 ZIP codes in our data, 7,025 have both Verizon and Xfinity reporting service, 15,663 have only Verizon, and 318 have only Xfinity. That means in many places you will only have one of the two, and the better choice is simply whichever one serves your address. Enter your ZIP on the home page to see which of them report service where you live.

Speed and connection type

Verizon is mostly Fios fiber and 5G Home; Xfinity is mostly cable and fiber. Fiber usually gives the most consistent speeds and upload performance, cable is widely available and fast on download, and DSL or fixed wireless can be slower and vary more by address. In the data, Verizon's top reported speed is 2.3 Gbps and Xfinity's is 2 Gbps, but that is the best tier in the best place, not what every address gets.

Price

Verizon: from $50/mo. 5G Home $50/mo with Auto Pay; $30-$35 with a Verizon mobile plan. Xfinity: from $20/mo. Watch for what happens when the promotional price ends, equipment fees, data caps and whether you need AutoPay or a bundle to get the low price.

Verizon: Verizon prices need Auto Pay. Fios or 5G Home depends on your address.

How to choose

  • If only one of them serves your address, that decides it. Check both pages: Verizon and Xfinity.
  • If both serve you, compare the total first-year cost including equipment and fees, not just the headline monthly price.
  • Pick the faster tier only if you need it. Most homes do well with 300 to 500 Mbps; see how much speed you need.
  • Ask for the price you will pay in month 13, and whether there is a contract or early termination fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is Verizon or Xfinity better?

It depends on which one serves your address and what you pay after the promotion. In our data both report service in 7,025 ZIP codes; elsewhere you may only have one.

Which is cheaper, Verizon or Xfinity?

Verizon lists from $50/mo and Xfinity lists from $20/mo for new customers. Compare the first-year total with equipment and fees.

Can I get Verizon and Xfinity at the same address?

Sometimes. 7,025 ZIP codes in our data have both reporting service, but availability is by address, so check each provider.

Which has faster internet, Verizon or Xfinity?

The top reported speeds are 2.3 Gbps for Verizon and 2 Gbps for Xfinity, but your address determines which speeds you can actually order.
