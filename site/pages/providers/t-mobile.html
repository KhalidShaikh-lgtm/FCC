T-Mobile: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

T-Mobile is a 5G Home Internet and fiber provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where T-Mobile reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, T-Mobile reports service in about 27,216 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (92%), across 50 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 27,216 ZIP codes with fixed wireless, 21 ZIP codes with fiber. Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with T-Mobile. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
