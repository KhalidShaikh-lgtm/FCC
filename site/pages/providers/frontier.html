Frontier: Support Numbers, Plans and Coverage

Frontier is a fiber and DSL provider. Here are its support phone numbers from its own site, the starting prices we track, and where it reports service.

Where Frontier reports service

In the provider-reported FCC availability data behind our ZIP pages, Frontier reports service in about 4,008 of the 29,663 ZIP codes we cover (14%), across 24 states. The most common connection types in that data are: 3,076 ZIP codes with DSL, 2,187 ZIP codes with fiber. The fastest speed reported anywhere is 7 Gbps download, but your address may get a lower tier or none at all.

Coverage is reported by ZIP code. Confirm your exact address with Frontier. Start at our internet by state pages or enter your ZIP on the home page.
