Internet Not Working: What to Do

When your internet goes down, most problems come from a stuck modem or router, a loose cable or a local outage. Work through these steps in order before you call.

Fix it in 10 steps

  1. Check whether it is just one device. Try another phone or computer. If only one device is offline, restart that device and forget and rejoin the Wi-Fi network.
  2. Look for an outage. Use your provider's app or website outage page, or ask a neighbor. If there is an outage, you may be owed a bill credit.
  3. Check the lights. Most modems show status lights. A light that is off, red or blinking for a long time usually points to a connection problem.
  4. Check the cables. Make sure the power cable, the coax or fiber cable and the Ethernet cable are firmly connected and not damaged.
  5. Restart your equipment. Unplug the modem and the router, wait 30 seconds, plug in the modem first, wait until its lights settle, then plug in the router.
  6. Test with a cable. Connect a computer to the router with an Ethernet cable. If that works, the problem is your Wi-Fi, not the internet connection.
  7. Check your account. A past-due bill or suspended account can cut service. Sign in to your provider's app to confirm the account is active.
  8. Reset only as a last resort. A factory reset erases your Wi-Fi name and password settings, so write them down first.
  9. Contact your provider. Have your account number ready, say what you already tried and ask for a ticket number.
  10. Ask for a technician if it keeps happening. Repeated drops often come from a faulty line, connector or old equipment, which a technician can fix.

When it keeps happening

Write down when the outages happen and for how long. A record helps you ask for a credit, a technician visit or a better plan. If the provider cannot fix it, check whether another provider serves your address on our ZIP check.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my internet not working but the Wi-Fi shows connected?

Your device can be connected to the router while the router has no connection to the internet. Restart the modem and router, then check the modem lights and your provider's outage page.

How long should I wait for an outage to end?

Many outages clear within a few hours, but if it lasts longer, contact your provider and ask about a bill credit.

Should I restart my router or my modem?

Restart both. Unplug the modem and router for 30 seconds, start the modem first and then the router.

Can bad weather knock out my internet?

Yes. Storms can damage lines or interrupt service, and heavy rain or snow can weaken satellite and fixed wireless signals.
